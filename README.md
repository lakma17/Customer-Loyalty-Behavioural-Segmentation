# Customer-Loyalty-Behavioural-Segmentation
python-jupyter-project

Summary
This study analyses the key drivers of customer loyalty and develops behavioural segmentation to support marketing decisions. Results show that loyalty is mainly driven by spending behaviour and predictive models confirm strong performance using behavioural factors. Customer segmentation identifies distinct groups such as VIP and low-value customers, but also reveals borderline cases with unstable cluster assignment. In hindsight, customer loyalty is best explained by behaviour and financial capacity, supporting behaviour-based segmentation over demographic approaches.
